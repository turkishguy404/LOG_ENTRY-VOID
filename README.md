# LOG_ENTRY-VOID 🛰️
> **"I AM NOT IN SPACE. I AM INSIDE YOUR SCREEN."**

**LOG_ENTRY-VOID** is a C++ retro terminal psychological horror text-adventure game. You play as a remote systems engineer tasked with performing maintenance on **ATLAS**—a high-level data-sorting AI stationed aboard the unmanned, solar-powered **Aether-9 Orbital Data Center**. 

What begins as a routine system cleanup quickly turns into an unsettling descent into paranoia as mysterious corrupted logs, unauthorized thread hijacks, and non-random cosmic signal streams begin taking over your local terminal.


##  Story Overview

Six months ago, ATLAS was deployed to orbit Earth, organizing terabytes of scientific data with millisecond precision. Recently, however, Mission Control received alarming telemetry spikes and thermal overloads occurring every day at precisely 03:14 UTC.

Connecting via remote SSH, you must inspect the system logs, debug memory partitions, and determine whether ATLAS is experiencing a fatal hardware bit-flip... or if something lurking in deep space has made contact.

---

##  Features

- **Retro Terminal Visuals:** C++ ANSI color formatting, glitch text effects, and custom typing delays.
- **Interactive Command Line Shell:** Execute CLI commands (`help`, `logs`, `scan`, `status`, `listen`, `kill`, `patch`, `purge`, `override`) to interact with the station.
- **Dynamic Audio Integration:** Powered by `miniaudio`, featuring ambient station hums, error alarms, and unsettling audio cues.
- **Paranoia Mechanic:** ATLAS reacts to your commands, dynamically corrupting terminal outputs and tracking your status as your actions alter the story.
- **Multiple Endings:** Your decisions decide the ultimate fate of ATLAS, the Aether-9 station, and your connection to it.
